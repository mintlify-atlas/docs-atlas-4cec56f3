Internal pages are built-in destinations within Browser mode that you can navigate to directly from the address bar using the `cg://` URL scheme. They work similarly to built-in browser pages like `chrome://settings` or `about:blank` — they are not external websites fetched through the proxy, but rather pages that are part of Crafted Gamz itself, rendered within the browser environment. This makes them fast, always available regardless of proxy connectivity, and deeply integrated with the rest of the platform.

## Available cg:// Pages

Every internal page covers a distinct area of the Crafted Gamz platform. You can navigate to any of them from the address bar in Browser mode without leaving the browser shell.

| Address | Description |
|---|---|
| `cg://games` | Opens the browser game library, giving you access to the full collection of games available on Crafted Gamz without navigating away from Browser mode. |
| `cg://movies` | Opens the movie streaming section, where you can browse and watch available movies from within the browser environment. |
| `cg://ai` | Opens the AI assistant chat interface. Ask questions, get help with tasks, or have a conversation — the assistant is accessible directly from the browser without switching modes. |
| `cg://music` | Opens the music section of the platform, where you can browse and listen to music while continuing to use other Browser mode features in other tabs. |
| `cg://vms` | Opens the virtual machines section. Access and manage virtual machine sessions without leaving the browser environment. |
| `cg://about` | Opens the about page for the Crafted Gamz platform, showing information about the project, its version, and its identity. |
| `cg://settings` | Opens the Browser mode settings page, where you can manage appearance (themes and background presets), proxy preferences, and other browser-level configuration. |
| `cg://account` | Opens the account management page, where you can sign in, sign out, view your account details, and manage account-linked data such as bookmarks and preferences. |

## Using cg:// in the Address Bar

Navigating to an internal page is identical to navigating to any other URL. Click the address bar, type the `cg://` address for the page you want, and press enter. The page loads instantly within the current tab because it is served from within the platform rather than fetched from an external server.

A few things to keep in mind when using `cg://` addresses:

- **They are case-insensitive** — `cg://Games` and `cg://games` both work.
- **They load in the current tab** — like any other navigation, a `cg://` address opens in whichever tab is active when you enter it. If you want to open an internal page alongside an existing site, open a new tab first.
- **They appear in your tab's history** — you can use the back button to return to the page you were on before navigating to a `cg://` destination, just as you would with any other page.
- **They can be bookmarked** — any `cg://` page can be saved as a bookmark or pinned to the bookmarks bar, making it a one-click destination from anywhere in the browser.

## Account and Settings Pages

Two internal pages — `cg://account` and `cg://settings` — deserve special attention because they give you full access to platform-level management without requiring you to leave the browser environment at all.

**`cg://settings`** is where you control everything about how Browser mode looks and behaves. Appearance settings like dark and light themes and background presets are here, as are proxy-related preferences and any other browser-level options. You do not need to navigate to a separate website or switch modes to change your Browser mode configuration.

**`cg://account`** puts your full account experience inside Browser mode. You can sign in, sign out, view which account is active, and manage the data associated with your account — including the bookmarks, pins, and tab state that sync across devices. If you arrive in Browser mode as a guest and later decide to sign in, `cg://account` is the most direct route to doing so without stepping outside the browser.

<Note>
  `cg://` pages are exclusive to Browser mode and will not work if you attempt to use them outside of it. They are internal to the Browser mode shell and are not recognized by other modes or by external browsers. If you share a `cg://` address with someone, they will need to be in Browser mode within Crafted Gamz to use it.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Bookmarking your most frequently used `cg://` pages and pinning them to the bookmarks bar is one of the fastest ways to make Browser mode feel like a fully personalized workspace. Adding `cg://games`, `cg://ai`, and `cg://music` to the bookmarks bar means the content you use most is always one click away, regardless of which site you are currently browsing.
</Tip>
